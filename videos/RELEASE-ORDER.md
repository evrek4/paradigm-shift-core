# Paradigm Shift Core - script library and release order

Ten scripts ready. Every one is 45-50s, four sections, and ends on a line that runs
back into its own hook, so nothing goes after the last word in the render.

hardest and where the channel's identity is clearest. Bring in deep tech and strategy once
subscribers exist, rather than mixing categories from day one.

## Prompt sheets

* prompts-02-03.md - videos 02 and 03, 8 prompts

Both are grouped G1 to G4 per video, in section order.

## House rules baked into every script

* No end card, CTA or subscribe frame. The closing line is written to feed the hook's
  first word, so anything after it breaks the loop.
* Visuals have no text in them. Captions are added at render time.
* Every script's Notes section lists the actual study and flags anything that is an
  argument rather than a finding, so nothing on the channel overstates its evidence.
